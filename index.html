Review a product variant change without losing the shopper

When a theme or app changes a product option control, the visible price and availability can update while a keyboard or screen-reader user receives no useful notice. This sample lets you change an option and inspect the resulting status.

Small selected. Available. Price $28.

What to check after a theme or app update

  1. Use Tab and arrow keys to change the option without a mouse.
  2. Check that the label names the option and the new price and availability are announced.
  3. Check that an unavailable option cannot be added and why.
  4. Repeat on the actual store. This sample cannot prove that a real product page, third-party app or checkout is accessible.

This is an educational sample, not a Shopify store or a test of a merchant.
